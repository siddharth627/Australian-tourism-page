Exploring Gold Coast

About The Gold Coast

The Gold Coast is a vibrant coastal city in Queensland, Australia, renowned for its 70 km of pristine beaches, world-famous surf breaks, and lush hinterland. Located just 66 km south of Brisbane, it is Australia's largest non-capital city, offering a perfect blend of high-energy nightlife and subtropical relaxation.

landmarks

Theme Parks: Located in the northern suburbs, the Gold Coast is Australia's theme park capital, home to Dreamworld, Warner Bros. Movie World, and Sea World.

Surfers Paradise The famous central hub featuring Cavill Avenue, dining, shopping, and golden sand beaches.

The Q1 Tower (Queensland Number One) is a 322.5-metre (1,058 ft) residential skyscraper in Surfers Paradise, Gold Coast, Australia. Completed in 2005, it is the tallest building in Australia and the second-tallest building in the Southern Hemisphere. Height 322.5 meters to the tip of its spire, making it the 3rd tallest free-standing structure in the Southern Hemisphere. Floors 78 residential levels, containing over 500 apartments, a day spa, and resort amenities. Design Inspiration Its sleek, curved glass shape pays homage to the Sydney Opera House and the Olympic torch from the 2000 Sydney Games.
