About Brisbane

Brisbane, the sun-drenched capital of Queensland, is Australia’s third-largest city, known for its vibrant subtropical outdoor lifestyle, a thriving arts scene, and the winding Brisbane River at its core. Originally known as Meanjin by the Turrbal and Yuggera peoples, it offers a laid-back, modern urban energy.

activities

Story Bridge Adventure Climb One of only three bridge climbs in the world where you can strap in and climb the structure for 360-degree views of the city, surrounding mountain ranges, and Moreton Bay.

The Wheel of Brisbane A giant, 60-meter tall observation wheel located in the heart of South Bank that offers air-conditioned, 360-degree views of the city's river bends and landmarks.
