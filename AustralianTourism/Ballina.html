About Ballina

Ballina is a laid-back coastal town in the Northern Rivers region of New South Wales, located about 740 km north of Sydney and just 20 minutes south of Byron Bay. Famous for its pristine beaches, calm waterways, and the iconic Big Prawn, it serves as a peaceful, family-friendly getaway.

Attractions in Ballina

Snap a photo with the 9-meter-tall Big Prawn or explore the Ballina Naval & Maritime Museum, which houses the famous Las Balsas raft.

outdoor activities

Enjoy cycling or walking along the scenic coastal path, go dolphin and whale watching from the headlands, or fish along the Richmond River.
